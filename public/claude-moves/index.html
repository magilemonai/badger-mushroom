Five Claude moves

The prompts from the video, ready to copy. Paste them into claude.ai. Moves one to four work on the free plan; the fifth is a pro tip for Max and Team subscribers.

Try them tonight

Move 1 · Talk to it

Start a new chat

Write a short limerick about a dog who is afraid of the mailman.

Move 2 · Talk back

Then reply in the same chat

Make it funnier, and make her a chihuahua.

Claude knows who "her" is because it's the same conversation. If your dog came out a "he," swap the word and it works the same.

Move 3 · Real details

Dinner from your fridge

Plan three weeknight dinners using mostly what I already have: [what's in your fridge right now]. Keep each under 30 minutes. Give me a short shopping list for anything missing. Keep the whole answer short.

Swap in your real list. The video used eggs, half a bag of spinach, a can of chickpeas, rice, a block of feta, two lemons, garlic, and some tired cherry tomatoes.

The five moves

  1. Talk to it. Type like you're texting a friend, with no magic words needed.
  2. Talk back. If something's off, reply in the same chat with what to change: "shorter," "warmer," "give me three options." Claude keeps the context, so you don't have to start over.
  3. Real details. What's in your fridge, who it's for, where you're going. You can add files and photos from the + button.
  4. Check the work. Hold what comes back against what you asked for, every time. Claude can be confidently wrong, and totals are a good place to look. In the video, a made-up expense summary said $63, and the items added up to $78.

Pro tip: the API credits in Max and Team

Since October 2026, Max and Team plans come with monthly credits for building on the Claude API. You only get them if you link a Claude Console organization.

How to claim them

  1. On claude.ai in a web browser, open Settings › Billing. On Team, it's Organization settings › Billing, and an Owner has to do it.
  2. In the API credits section, pick the Console organization that should get the credits and link it. You can create one there if you don't have one.
  3. The credits show up under Promotional credits on the Billing page at platform.claude.com. Make an API key and start building.

The fine print

  • They don't roll over. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.
  • They cover the Claude API, Managed Agents, the Agent SDK and the Console playground. They don't cover Claude Code or extra usage in the Claude apps.
  • New subscribers can claim after 7 days on the plan.
  • You can't switch the linked organization yourself (support can), so link the one you'll actually build in.

Source: API credits for Max and Team plans, Anthropic's docs. Checked October 26, 2026. Plans change, so the docs page wins if it says something different.

Before you paste anything

  • Paste only what you'd be comfortable putting in an email. Leave out passwords, account numbers, and other people's private details.
  • Decide whether your chats can help train future models: Settings, then Privacy, then "Help improve our AI models." Either choice is fine. Pick it on purpose.
  • Claude can be confidently wrong. For anything that matters (money, health, dates), check the facts yourself.
