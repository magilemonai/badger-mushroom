What it means

The numbers and where they came from
All figures are the Board of Elections' unofficial check-in counts at close of polls, posted nightly on @BOENYC and relayed by NY1, NBC New York, Patch, Spectrum, amNY and CBS. Cells marked * are derived by subtraction from neighboring posts; blank cells were not found in published sources. Registered-voter counts are the BOE's active enrollment as of June 2026. Poll hours differed by day (weekends 9–5, Tue/Wed 10–8, Thu 8–4), and 2025's Day 6 fell during a torrential downpour. The map is a simplified sketch of the five boroughs, not a survey.
