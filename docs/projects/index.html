A Valisar tarot battler

Hue Goes There?

A color-mixing tower defense

Paint blots ooze toward your Prism, and a sprout can only hurt the ones that share a pigment with it. Sprouts are also walls, so every one you plant bends the path. Fuse two neighbors to blend a new color, which frees a tile and reopens the maze. Route the blots through a paint puddle and they change color on the way. Eight gardens, and something very large waiting in the last one. Playable on desktop and mobile. Built with Claude Code.

GameTower DefenseClaude Code

Tray Bien

A waiter game where things keep going wrong

You're Gerald, a waiter at the city's fourth-tallest restaurant. Catch what's on a plate and stack it up to the bell while a deadpan narrator reports each new problem: geese in the forecast, a visit from the moon, a health inspector who makes even the falling soup hold still. Nineteen of those, every one read aloud. Playable on desktop and mobile. Built with Claude Code, narrated by an ElevenLabs voice.

GameArcadeClaude Code

Rock Flock

A rhythm game for birds on a wire

A rhythm game where the telephone wire is the hit line. Four birds dive down their lanes and you land each one on the beat. Every landing plays a note of the melody, so a miss leaves a hole in the tune. Three original songs about bird stuff, and a cat that climbs the pole when you slip. Playable on desktop and mobile. Built with Claude Code.

GameRhythmClaude Code

Shatterhour

An exploding clock-tower city

A city built on a giant clock face, caught mid-explosion. You ride the clock hand: sweep it to run time forward or back, and every full turn puts the city back together in the next of four dimensions. Runs in the browser on desktop and mobile. Built with Claude Code in three.js.
