Five Claude moves

The prompts from the video, ready to copy. Paste them into claude.ai. Moves one to four work on the free plan; the fifth is a pro tip for Max and Team subscribers.

Try them tonight

Move 1 · Talk to it

Start a new chat

Write a short limerick about a dog who is afraid of the mailman.

Move 2 · Talk back

Then reply in the same chat

Make it funnier, and make her a chihuahua.

Claude knows who "her" is because it's the same conversation. If your dog came out a "he," swap the word and it works the same.

Move 3 · Real details

Dinner from your fridge

Plan three weeknight dinners using mostly what I already have: [what's in your fridge right now]. Keep each under 30 minutes. Give me a short shopping list for anything missing. Keep the whole answer short.

Swap in your real list. The video used eggs, half a bag of spinach, a can of chickpeas, rice, a block of feta, two lemons, garlic, and some tired cherry tomatoes.

The five moves

  1. 01

    Talk to it.

    Type like you're texting a friend, with no magic words needed.

  2. 02

    Talk back.

    If something's off, reply in the same chat with what to change: "shorter," "warmer," "give me three options." Claude keeps the context, so you don't have to start over.

  3. 03

    Real details.

    What's in your fridge, who it's for, where you're going. You can add files and photos from the + button.

  4. 04

    Check the work.

    Hold what comes back against what you asked for, every time. Claude can be confidently wrong, and totals are a good place to look. In the video, a made-up expense summary said $63, and the items added up to $78.

  5. 05

    Claim your API credits.Pro tip

    If you're on Max or Team, your plan includes monthly credits for the Claude API. Details below.

How to claim them

  1. On claude.ai in a web browser, open Settings › Billing. On Team, it's Organization settings › Billing, and an Owner has to do it.
  2. In the API credits section, pick the Console organization that should get the credits and link it. You can create one there if you don't have one.
  3. The credits show up under Promotional credits on the Billing page at platform.claude.com. Make an API key and start building.

The fine print

  • They don't roll over. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.
  • They cover the Claude API, Managed Agents, the Agent SDK and the Console playground. They don't cover Claude Code or extra usage in the Claude apps.
  • New subscribers can claim after 7 days on the plan.
  • You can't switch the linked organization yourself (support can), so link the one you'll actually build in.

Make your own

A prompt for a video like this one

Claude Code made this video: it wrote the script and the animation code in Remotion, a tool for making videos with code, and the voice comes from ElevenLabs. Here's a starting prompt for making your own. You'll need Claude Code and a computer with Node.js installed. Expect a few rounds of notes after the first draft; mine took several.

Paste into Claude Code

A 60-second explainer

I want to make a 60-second vertical video that teaches [your topic] to [who it's for]. Think social media short: packed with useful information, and fun to watch. Build it in code with Remotion, in a new folder. 1. Script first. Write about 150 spoken words in short numbered lines, one idea per line, with a hook in the first three seconds. Shape it as [five quick tips]. Show me the script before you build anything. 2. Keep it real. Any answer, number or product detail on screen has to be real: generate it or look it up, and tell me the source. Label anything made up as made up, on screen. 3. Voice: use ElevenLabs if I give you an API key, otherwise a placeholder voice so we can check the timing. Time the captions and animations to the words. 4. Style: 1080x1920, big captions of 1-3 words that work with the sound off, a title card for each point, quick cuts on a steady beat, music under the voice, and an end card that recaps every point. Keep the important stuff in the middle of the frame so it also crops to 4:5 for LinkedIn. 5. Before the final render, show me a still from each section. Then export a 9:16 and a 4:5 version, with the audio leveled for social. Ask me what you need to know before you start.

Fill in the colored parts first. Remotion is free for individuals, non-profits and companies with up to three employees; bigger companies need its company license.

Before you paste anything

  • Paste only what you'd be comfortable putting in an email. Leave out passwords, account numbers, and other people's private details.
  • Decide whether your chats can help train future models: Settings, then Privacy, then "Help improve our AI models." Either choice is fine. Pick it on purpose.
  • Claude can be confidently wrong. For anything that matters (money, health, dates), check the facts yourself.
