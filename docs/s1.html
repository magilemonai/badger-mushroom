What's in the filing

The numbers all six share

Every number in the videos comes from the S-1 itself, with page numbers so you can check them.

Revenue
$16.3 billion in the first half of 2026, up from $1.2 billion in the first half of 2025. (p. 22)
The loss
$80.7 billion over the same six months. Almost all of it is two non-cash accounting marks. Notes that convert into stock (plus a few similar instruments) got more valuable as the company did, and the books record that as a $72.4 billion loss; a second mark on other financial instruments adds $8.7 billion. The operating loss was $693 million, and $1.1 billion of interest income offset part of the total. Cash from operations was positive: $3.9 billion. (p. 22 and footnote 2; cash flow statement)
Customers
About 250 customers spent $10 million or more each over the twelve months to September 30, and more than 1,900 spent $1 million or more. (pp. 8–9)
Who controls it
Shares sold to the public (Class A) get one vote each. A single Class F share, issued to an LLC of the seven co-founders, carries 50.1% of shareholder votes on general matters. A separate Long-Term Benefit Trust can elect most of the board. (pp. 12–15)
The fund
About 847 million non-voting shares, 5% of the company on a fully diluted basis, are set aside in the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a subsidiary the company owns. (p. 12)
The risk
“Our AI models may behave in unintended, misaligned, or autonomous ways, including by taking harmful actions that no user or third party directed.” (p. 16)
The listing
Ticker ANT on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. The price and the share count are still blank. (cover page)
